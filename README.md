## Our product

### ApexByte Grid

ApexByte Grid is our developer-infrastructure product focused on API endpoint monitoring, webhook delivery visibility and incident investigation.

The product is being developed to help teams:

- Monitor the availability and response behaviour of endpoints they own or are authorised to test.
- Review webhook delivery activity and related outcomes.
- Investigate failures using operational history and incident context.

ApexByte Grid is designed around an evidence-led workflow. AI assistance is intended to be optional; the core workflow does not depend on AI-generated output.
